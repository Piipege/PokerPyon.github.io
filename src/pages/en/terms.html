4. Playing with friends

  • Tables with friends currently run without our server: the browser of the player who created the table (the host) deals the cards and runs the bots, and the others connect to it directly.
  • Technically the host's browser knows every player's cards. The deck is fair: card fingerprints are published before the deal, and every player can verify the cards that are revealed. Play with people you trust.
  • If the host closes the tab or loses the connection, the game at the table stops.
  • The stats that other players see at the table are reported by each player about themselves and may be inaccurate.
