Refund Policy

Version 1.0, 13 November 2026. This version covers payments by international cards and cryptocurrency. Ruble payments: see the Russian version.

All PyonPoker features are currently free and no payments are accepted. Below is how refunds will work once paid features appear. The exact terms will be in the offer we publish before accepting payments.

1. What may be sold

  • Cosmetics: card backs, tables, chip sets, avatars, emotes, seasonal sets.
  • Subscriptions, such as an extended coach and cloud stats.

Purchases give no gameplay advantage. Game chips are never sold for money and cannot be cashed out.

2. Seller

Payments by international cards and cryptocurrency are accepted by an individual entrepreneur registered in Georgia. The seller's details will be published in the offer before payments start.

3. When we refund

  • Not delivered or not working because of us, and not fixed within a reasonable time: full refund.
  • Charged twice or charged the wrong amount: the excess is refunded.
  • Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time: they will not renew, and access remains until the end of the paid period.
  • Right of withdrawal (EU/EEA and similar laws). Digital content and services are delivered immediately after payment. At checkout we will ask for your consent to immediate delivery and your acknowledgement that the 14-day right of withdrawal is lost once delivery starts. If you have not used a purchase, ask us within 14 days and we will refund it.
  • Cosmetics that have been delivered, used and work as described are otherwise not refundable. This does not affect your statutory rights.

4. Cryptocurrency payments

  • An approved refund is paid in the same currency and network you paid with (for example USDT on TON, TON, BTC or XMR), for the amount you paid in that currency, minus the network fee.
  • You will need to give us a refund address that you control. Payments sent to the wrong address, with the wrong memo or in the wrong network may be impossible to recover.
  • Underpayments are refunded or can be topped up. Overpayments are refunded on request.

5. How to request a refund

  1. Contact us through the official PyonPoker channels listed on the website. Include the date, amount and order number (or transaction ID for crypto).
  2. We reply within 10 days.
  3. Card refunds go back to the same card. The bank may take up to 10 business days.

Purchases made in apps from Google Play or the App Store are refunded under those stores' rules.

6. Your rights

This policy does not limit the rights that you have under the mandatory consumer protection law of your country.
