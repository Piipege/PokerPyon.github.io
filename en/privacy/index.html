Privacy Policy

Version 1.0, 13 November 2026. This version applies to users outside Russia. Users in Russia: see the Russian version.

In short. PyonPoker currently has no accounts and no server of ours that collects your data. Your settings, name and stats stay in your browser. When you play with friends, they see your name, avatar, chat messages and actions at the table. We use no cookies and no advertising trackers.

1. Who is responsible

The owner of the PyonPoker service (pyonpoker.com). This policy explains how the service handles information about you, in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Law of Georgia on Personal Data Protection.

2. What stays in your browser

This data is stored only on your device (localStorage and IndexedDB) and is not sent to us:

  • settings: language, theme, sound, keys, table and card look;
  • your table name and avatar;
  • your hand history and stats, course progress and drill records;
  • technical data: a random player ID, a secret seat key, the code of your last table, and the host's table state (so a table can resume after a page reload).

You can delete it with "Clear statistics" in your profile or by clearing the site data in your browser settings. Once cleared, it cannot be recovered.

3. What other players see

When you sit at a table with friends or watch a game, the other participants receive:

  • your name, avatar and actions at the table, and your cards if you show them;
  • your chat messages and emotes;
  • a short summary of your stats (hands, VPIP, PFR and so on), if you have any.

Your hidden cards go only to the host's browser, which deals the cards. If a table is public, its name, blinds and number of players appear in the shared table list.

Legal basis (GDPR): connecting you to a table you chose to join is necessary to provide the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b)). Keeping data in your own browser is under your control.

5. Cookies, analytics, advertising

We use no cookies, advertising or trackers. In the future we may add cookieless analytics that collects no personal data (such as Umami), to see overall visits and popular sections. We will update this policy first.

6. Device permissions

Notifications, vibration, "keep screen on" and "Share" work only if you turn them on, and only on your device. The evening recap picture is made in your browser.

7. Age

The service is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Accounts and payments in the future

If accounts, a cloud profile or paid features appear, we will publish a new version of this policy before they launch. It will say what is collected, where and for how long it is stored, the legal basis, and who the controller is. Card details are processed by payment providers and will never reach us.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR and Georgian law you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to complain to a supervisory authority (in Georgia, the Personal Data Protection Service). Today all your data is in your browser, so you control it directly: view it in your profile, clear your stats, or delete the site data.
